# Video formula coverage

Reference: Matharium video `JmivLg-1vTzbhlxE.mp4` (not bundled). All 10 numbered scenes were visually checked at the timestamps below.

The original collection contained related ingredients, but none of the ten complete constructions as an exact preset. Eight visible constructions are now implemented; two layered ones are clearly labeled reconstructions because the video omits the melody list `c`. Both the low trace and high melody are drawn and played simultaneously.

| Video scene | Earlier coverage | Added result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 00:02 · The kick roll | Exponential pulse train. Missing the variable (k,p) schedule. | Visible equation implemented; equality at x mod 4 = 3 assigned to the final branch. |
| 2 · 00:08 · The cowbell | Decay and stepped contours existed separately. Missing the simultaneous second voice and block offsets. | Two-voice reconstruction: the video does not supply the c melody list. |
| 3 · 00:14 · The quantized sine | Quantized sine. Existing family quantizes a single sine with rounding. | Visible two-sine construction implemented with floor quantization of both time and height. |
| 4 · 00:20 · The shapeshifter | Sawtooth, triangle, and decay separately. Missing the exact piecewise combination. | All three branches implemented. |
| 5 · 00:26 · A whole drum beat | Generic rhythm and envelope families. Missing the alternating branches and silent intervals. | All visible branches implemented; undefined intervals are silent. |
| 6 · 00:38 · The staircase | Other staircase families. Missing the exact triangle quantizer and offset list. | Visible equation and c list implemented. |
| 7 · 00:42 · Saturation | Tanh saturation and arctangent terraces. Missing the normalized arctangent sine and four-stage list. | Visible equation and s list implemented. |
| 8 · 00:45 · The fractal | Weierstrass texture. Existing family has fixed term count and different scale. | Visible coefficients and staged inclusive N = (1,2,3,4) implemented. |
| 9 · 00:54 · The chirp | Quadratic and quantized waves separately. Missing the growing quantized quadratic chirp. | Visible floor expression and p = x + 8 implemented. |
| 10 · 01:01 · The final boss | No layered equivalent. Missing the combination of #1 and #2. | Two-voice reconstruction with the same disclosed replacement melody as #2. |

The replacement c list (zero-based): (0,3,7,5,3,0,3,5,7,10,8,7,5,3,0,−2,0,3,7,5,3,0,−2,0,3,5,7,5,3,0,−2,0). This is original material, not a recovered list from the video.

For the four-block lists, the app uses −4 ≤ x < 12 with one-based b = floor(x/4)+2, holding the fourth block at x=12. Other examples retain −8…8. At the kick-roll boundary x mod 4 = 3, which is omitted by the video’s strict inequalities, the app uses the final branch. The drum-beat gaps are undefined in the graph and silent in audio.

The video’s formulas and coefficients are retained; audio is generated by Sonograph’s own synthesizer, so these presets do not promise identical recorded sound.

The browser-readable audit with equations, definitions, and direct preset links is in [dist/video-formulas.html](dist/video-formulas.html).
